# Business Thoughts - How I've Won Previously

1: Be willing to do what others are not.
2: Empower people to make money. 

**That's it.**

1 Find what others arent willing to do and target it
1.1 Too scared or fear of punishment
1.2 Too gritty or brutal. fear of pain.
1.3 Too morrally ambigious. fear of judgement.

2 Find a way to put money in their pocket, then sell the dream.
2.1 Leave money on the table, dont max your own profit.
2.2 Teach them the system on how to profit and win (but not the whole playbook)
2.3 Put them in power. Hand them customers.

Number 1 is a differentiator and wedge.
Number 2 is lockdown and loyalty.
