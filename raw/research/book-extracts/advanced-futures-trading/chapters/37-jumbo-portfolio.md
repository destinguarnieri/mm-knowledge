# Appendix C: The Jumbo portfolio

## Purpose and central point

This appendix is the complete instrument specification for the author's **Jumbo portfolio**: 102 futures instruments, arranged by asset class in Tables 172–183. It is a reference list rather than a discussion of trading rules, formulas, examples, or portfolio-construction procedures.

Each entry identifies the instrument, the author's broker-specific market code, the exchange used, trading currency, futures multiplier, and the first year of available data in the author's data set.

## Scope, conventions, and limitations

- **Market code** is the code used by the author's futures broker. It can differ across brokers and from the official exchange code.
- **Exchange** is the exchange on which the author trades the instrument. The instrument may also trade elsewhere.
- **Multiplier** is the futures multiplier used by the author. Other contract variants with different multipliers may be available.
- **First year with data** means the first year in the author's data set—not the date trading began. For some micro/mini futures, earlier data may have been backfilled from larger contracts; thus the listed date may precede or follow the launch of that particular contract.
- The appendix supplies no formula, unit definition for a multiplier, position-sizing method, rationale for selection, or method for combining the 102 instruments. Those details are therefore not inferable from this appendix alone.

## Relationships, practical use, and cautions

- The portfolio spans bonds/interest rates, equities, volatility, FX, metals/crypto, energy, and agricultural markets; the appendix explicitly groups instruments by these asset classes.
- For implementation, the market code should be treated as broker-specific, while the exchange and multiplier identify the particular setup used by the author. They must not be assumed to generalize unchanged to another broker or contract version.
- The first-data-year field is a **data-availability boundary**, not a reliable start-of-trading date. Backfilled micro/mini series are specifically called out as an exception.
- There are no figures, diagrams, formulas, derivations, worked examples, footnotes, or explicit links to other chapters in this appendix.

## Key takeaways

1. The Jumbo portfolio is a 102-instrument futures universe documented by Tables 172–183.
2. Every instrument is specified by broker code, exchange, currency, multiplier, and first available data year.
3. The data dates and codes require careful interpretation: they are author- and broker-specific, and should not be substituted for contract launch dates or official exchange identifiers.

## Glossary

- **Jumbo portfolio:** The author's 102-instrument futures portfolio listed in this appendix.
- **Market code:** The author's futures-broker code for an instrument.
- **Futures multiplier:** The multiplier used by the author for the listed futures instrument.
- **First year with data:** First appearance in the author's data set; not necessarily the contract's first trading year.
- **Backfilled data:** Historical prices supplied from a larger contract for some micro or mini futures, as noted by the author.
