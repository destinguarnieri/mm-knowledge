# Appendix A: Resources

## Purpose and central message

This appendix is a curated set of further-reading references and website links relevant to futures trading, systematic trading, data, execution, and the book's supporting materials. It does **not** introduce a trading model, formula, procedure, or worked example. Its practical function is to direct the reader to supplementary learning, market infrastructure, data sources, and the author's own materials.

## Further reading

| Work | Author(s) | Publication details given | What the appendix says it is useful for |
|---|---|---|---|
| *Leveraged Trading* | Robert Carver | 2019, Harriman House | The author's third book; described as essential reading for traders learning the basics of futures trading. |
| *Options, Futures, and Other Derivatives* | John Hull | 2017, Pearson | A fairly technical introduction to futures and other derivatives. |
| *Fortune’s Formula: The Untold Story of the Scientific Betting System That Beat the Casinos and Wall Street* | William Poundstone | 2006, Hill and Wang | A non-technical, enjoyable introduction to the Kelly criterion. |
| *Following the Trend* | Andreas F. Clenow | 2012, Wiley | Provides more detail specifically about trend following. |
| *Expected Returns: An Investor’s Guide to Harvesting Market Rewards* | Antti Ilmanen | 2011, Wiley | Introduces risk premia and supplies a “shopping list” of premia to use in trading strategies. |
| *Efficiently Inefficient: How Smart Money Invests and Market Prices Are Determined* | Lasse H. Pedersen | 2015, Princeton University Press | Gives more detail on several strategies in this book, plus sections on execution, backtesting, performance measurement, and risk premium. |
| *The Rise of Carry: The Dangerous Consequences of Volatility Suppression and the New Financial Order of Decaying Growth and Recurring Crisis* | Tim Lee, Jamie Lee, and Kevin Coldiron | 2019, McGraw Hill | A book about carry; characterized as more of a philosophical inquiry than a trading manual. |
| *Algorithmic Trading: Winning Strategies and Their Rationale* | Ernie Chan | 2013, Wiley | Covers certain key strategies, with particular strength in mean reversion. |
| *STIR Futures: Trading Euribor and Eurodollar Futures* | Stephen Aikin | 2012, Harriman House | Strong on relative-value calendar trades in short-term interest-rate futures (STIR). |
| *Systematic Trading* | Robert Carver | 2015, Harriman House | The author's first book; described as essential reading for traders developing their own trading systems. |
