## Entry format

Keep a short ordered list when priorities are selected. For each goal, record:

- **Outcome:** the result we want and why it matters.
- **Done when:** observable completion criteria.
- **State:** proposed, active, or paused; include the selection/change date.
- **Context:** link to the relevant project or domain page for scope, constraints, and execution detail.

Only Destin's selection makes a proposed goal active. Do not invent targets or deadlines. When a goal completes or is dropped, record the decision in the changelog and remove it from the selected list. Goals do not authorize live operations or replace the current request.
