# American Venture Fund

The purpose is giving the American People access to broad exposure of American innovation of privately held, Venture backed companies. 

The result is we create a Venture Industry that transforms the Power Law nature of historic returns by creating a structural liquidity system.

The fund is inteded to be a public vehicle that trades on an American Exchange.(NYSE/NASDAQ)

*The American Venture Fund may be reffered to as "AVF" or "the fund" is this document*

## Operations
The fund has two primary modes of operandi. 

1. Liquidity Provisioning to Venture Funds.
2. Secondary Share Purchases. 

LP: The fund allocates broadly accross sectors to American Venture firms raising capital for new funds. 

Secondary Desk: AVF has an open trade desk to allow early investors and employees to get liquid in a systematic and structal fashion. 
This allows the fund to accummulate direct exposure to high quality companies that are provably succeeding and raising new financing. 

The duality of the funds allocation provides both breadth and depth of exposure, the former from the LP and latter from the Secondary Desk.
