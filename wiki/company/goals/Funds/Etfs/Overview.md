# Sector ETFS
The core offerings have three family groups, Alpha, Beta, Theta. 

The product set are Sector Etfs benchmarked against legacy Sector Etf providers and underlying indexes.

For each sector we aim to provide 1 etf per family group totaling 3 per sector.

We charge fees strictly based on the outperformance over the benchmark. 

The funds are primarily systematic and automated, with light human touch on Theta products.


# Broad Markets
The core offerings aim to provide broad market exposure through systematically managed funds.

HOT30, HOT100, HOT500, HOT1000

# PRODUCTS

- Alpha funds are cross sectional, targeting both long and short exposure to the top/bottom percentiles of the sector. 
- Beta Funds are uni-directional, targeting long exposure to the assets out performing the sector index.
- Theta funds harvest yield premium by targeting out of the money covered options of the underlying stocks in the sector.
- HOTLIST™️ funds are long only actively managed
