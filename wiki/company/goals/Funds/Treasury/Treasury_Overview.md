# Money Machine Treasury Funds Overview

Treasury Funds are permanent capital structures that accummulate pristine assets to provide durable capital appreciation.

Stablility, Safety, Long Duration capital appreciation are the core common mandates across Treasuries.


## Differences from ETFS

Treasury Funds operate with a balance sheet.

The funds may tender debt notes to accumulate assets. 

The operation of the Treasury is governed by an semi-independent Board of Directors. 

Suggested director selection based on 1 exec from each major Financial company. (JPM,Morgan,Blackrock,Goldman,...)
*A Money Machine Executive is required to be the Chairman of the Board for all Money Machine issued Treasury Funds.*

## Funding Sources

Funding sources are primary composed of three main drivers:

1. Direct Prime Inflows
2. Bond Issuance
3. Money Machine Core Cashflows

## Primary Operation

-Asset Selection
-Asset Allocation Weights and Rebalancing
-Bond Pricing
-Inflation Rate
