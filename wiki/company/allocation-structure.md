# Allocation Structure

## Target Capital Allocation

- 30% Treasury
- 50% Spot
- 10% Low Leverage
- 10% High Leverage

## Treasury Allocation

Treasury Funds are permanent capital structures that accummulate pristine assets to provide durable capital appreciation. Stablility, Safety, Long Duration capital appreciation are the core common mandates across Treasuries.

## Spot Allocation

Spot asset strategies target modest returns that absorb large amounts of liquidity. 
They inteded to capture both slow-tempo alpha that occurs over days/weeks and fast-tempo market gyrations. 
The universe consist of all stocks and ETFs that are traded on the NYSE, NASDAQ, NYSE ARCA, and Crypto exchanges.

## Low Leverage Allocation

Low leverage strategies target medium time horizons with modest volatility.
Assets universe consist of futures, crypto, commodities, and other assets that leveraged by nature.

## High Leverage Allocation

High leverage strategies target short time horizons with high volatility.
Assets universe consist of futures, crypto, commodities, and other assets that are leveraged by nature.

### Rebalancing Strategy

The goal is to move captial down the risk spectrum over time.
Rebalancing occurs on a trade by trade basis, ensuring that the portfolio is always protecting profits.
